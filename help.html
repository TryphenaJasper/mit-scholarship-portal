01

How eligibility checking works

Your profile is compared against the eligibility criteria of each scholarship.

✓

Profile-based evaluation

The portal checks relevant information such as CGPA, HSC percentage, attendance, arrears, parental income, branch, year of study, gender, community and other scholarship status.

Academic

CGPA & HSC

Some scholarships use academic performance such as HSC marks or CGPA.

Financial

Family income

Several scholarships specify a maximum parental or family income.

Academic status

Attendance & arrears

Certain scholarships require minimum attendance and no arrears for renewal.

Personal

Profile conditions

Gender, community, disability status, school background and other conditions may apply.

02

What is Gap Analysis?

Find out what you need to improve for scholarships you are close to qualifying for.

GAP

Turn eligibility criteria into actionable feedback

Instead of simply saying that you are not eligible, Gap Analysis identifies measurable academic requirements that you have not yet reached.

Example Current CGPA: 6.8

Required CGPA: 7.0

You need 0.2 more CGPA.

Financial information such as family income is displayed as a scholarship criterion, but it is not treated as an improvement gap.

03

What is Priority Resolution?

Find the best compatible combination of scholarships.

MAX

Maximize your scholarship benefit

Some scholarships cannot be combined because their conditions say that the student should not apply for or receive another scholarship. Priority Resolution considers these conflicts before recommending a combination.

Scholarship A ₹25,000
+
Scholarship B ₹20,000
=
Total value ₹45,000

The actual recommendation will depend on eligibility, scholarship conflicts, limits, deadlines and the student's profile.
